## Ordering Additional Services

Demers Events & Expo Services is the official show services partner for FlightSimExpo. For equipment beyond what's listed in the Partner Hub—such as furniture, labor, graphics, and more—see the Demers Service Kit.

<Warning>
  The deadline to receive discounted pricing through Demers is **Friday, May 28**. Orders placed after the deadline will be charged at floor rates.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The exhibit hall floor is **carpeted**. Ordering additional carpet for your booth is not recommended.
</Note>

## About Basic Furnishings

<Info>
  Bring any accessories, tools, and peripherals you'll need to set up your booth, including extension cords, power adaptors, computer peripherals, tape, etc. FlightSimExpo does not stock these items, and anything needed on-site must be sourced locally at your own expense.

  We strongly recommend doing a "dry run" of your booth setup before shipping items or traveling to the show, to make sure you have everything you need.
</Info>

### Pipe and Drape

All booths include pipe and drape as noted on the floorplan. Booths with rear drape also include a sign with the exhibitor's name and booth number.

### Electrical

All electrical drops include a power bar with 6 NEMA 5-15R outlets (120, 3 prong). Exhibitors are responsible for bringing adaptors and extension cords as needed.

Electrical drops will be placed at the back of your booth. Email any specific layout diagrams to phil@flightsimassociation.com.
