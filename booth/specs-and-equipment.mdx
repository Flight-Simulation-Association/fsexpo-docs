## Ordering Additional Services

Demers Events & Expo Services is the official show services partner for FlightSimExpo. For equipment beyond what's listed in the Partner Hub—such as furniture, labor, graphics, and more—see the Demers Service Kit.

<Warning>
  The deadline to receive discounted pricing through Demers is **Friday, May 28**. Orders placed after the deadline will be charged at floor rates.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The exhibit hall floor is **carpeted**. Ordering additional carpet for your booth is not recommended.
</Note>

### Placing an order through Demers
<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the Demers Exhibitor Kit">
    See the full range of offerings. **Link to Demers Exhibitor Kit.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Email info@demersexpo.com">
    Ask them to "create an online ordering login for FlightSimExpo 2026."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order through the online storefront">
    They'll send you a login link to view their catalog and place an order. Orders placed online save the 8% administrative fee.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Exhibit Tables

Standard exhibit tables are 6' long × 2' wide × 30" tall, with a 2" lip, black skirting, and a white top.

## Before you ship or travel

<Tip>
  Bring any accessories, tools, and peripherals you'll need to set up your booth — extension cords, power adaptors, computer peripherals, tape, etc. FlightSimExpo does not stock these items, and anything needed on-site must be sourced locally at your own expense.

  We strongly recommend doing a **"dry run"** of your booth setup before shipping items or traveling to the show, to make sure you have everything you need.
</Tip>
