## Before the Show

Start promoting long before the doors open! Exhibitors typically use a variety of methods based on what works best for their audience. A few ideas that are typically effective are listed below.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Post Regularly" icon="share-nodes">
    Don't just post once—build a "hype train". Share behind-the-scenes prep, booth sneak peeks, and reminders as the show gets closer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Email Your Customers" icon="envelope-open-text">
    Let your customers and newsletter subscribers know you'll be at FlightSimExpo, where to find your booth, and what to expect.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Tease a Reveal" icon="sparkles">
    Save an announcement, product launch, or update for the show itself, and tease it in advance to give people a reason to stop by.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cross-Promote" icon="retweet">
    Share and comment on FlightSimExpo's own announcements. It helps our audience see your involvement early and puts you on their radar.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Use the Official Hashtag" icon="hashtag">
    Tag your posts so attendees (and we) can find them. It's an easy way to get re-shared by FlightSimExpo and other partners.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Offer a Show-Only Perk" icon="star">
    A discount code, unique activation, or bonus only available at FlightSimExpo gives people an extra reason to visit your booth in person.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
