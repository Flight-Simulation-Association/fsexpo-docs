## Sell Yourself

Selling your own products at FlightSimExpo doesn't require any registration or paperwork in advance. If you're selling tangible products, just tell us you're doing so. We'll hand you a **one-time sales tax return** to fill out on-site before departing the event. We'll invoice you for the applicable sales tax and remit it to the State of Nevada on your behalf. 

As of January 2024, the applicable sales tax rate is **8.38%**.

<Note>
  If you sell at more than two events in Nevada within a twelve-month period, you're required to register with the Department of Taxation and obtain your own Sales & Use Tax Permit.
</Note>
