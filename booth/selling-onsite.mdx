Attendees consistently tell us they want the option to buy on the show floor! This year, it's easier than ever to offer products to attendees. Also, consider pre-sales with on-site pickup or point-of-sale with shipping to the attendee's home.

## Option 1: Sell Items Yourself
If you are selling tangible products at FlightSimExpo, we will issue you a “one-time sales tax return” to complete on-site following the event. You will remit applicable sales tax to us, and we will remit it to the State of Nevada on your behalf. As of January 2024, the applicable sales tax rate is 8.38%.

If you sell at more than two events in Nevada in a twelve-month period, you must register with the Department of Taxation and obtain your own Sales & Use Tax Permit. Please reach out with questions.

## Option 2: Sell Through FlightsimWebshop

<Info>
  This option is available for hardware vendors only.
</Info>

FlightsimWebshop is a leading flight simulation hardware retailer that will be selling hardware on-site at FlightSimExpo. FlightsimWebshop will handle state registration and collect sales tax on your behalf. Please reach out to jeremy@flightsimwebshop.com for more information.
