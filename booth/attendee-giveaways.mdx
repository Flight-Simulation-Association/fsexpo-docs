We encourage partners to bring giveaway items for attendees. In the past, we've seen branded mugs, T-shirts, hats, pens, bags, and more given out to all FlightSimExpo attendees.

- Giveaway items for **exhibitors** are placed at the exhibitor's booth.
- Items for **non-exhibiting sponsors** are placed near registration.

<Tip>
  FlightSimExpo will cover material handling charges for attendee giveaway items for **Platinum and Diamond Sponsors**, provided they're shipped and addressed correctly.
</Tip>

## How many should I bring?

<Card title="Recommended quantity: 1,500" icon="hashtag">
  FlightSimExpo 2025 had almost 2,500 in-person attendees. For 2026, we recommend a quantity of **1,500** for giveaway items.
</Card>

## Logistics

You can either bring items with you or ship them in advance.

<Warning>
  Shipped items require special addressing and coordination with FlightSimExpo so you aren't charged material handling. Items must be received between **May 12 and May 29**.
</Warning>

Email **phil@flightsimassociation.com** for attendee giveaway shipping instructions.
