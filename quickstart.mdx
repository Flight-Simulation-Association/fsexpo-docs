This page covers critical items every exhibitor needs to complete before June. Each step links to the full details if you need them.

<Steps>
<Step title="Book Your Hotel and Travel">
    The sooner you book, the better. Prices only increase as the event gets closer. FlightSimExpo has negotiated discounts on hotels, air travel, and ground transportation.

    See [Hotel & Travel Discounts](/travel/hotel-and-travel-discounts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Order Booth Add-ons">
    Use the Partner Hub to see what's included in your booth (furnishings, internet, electrical, monitors) and order anything extra.

    See [Booth Specs & Equipment](/booth/specs-and-equipment).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide How You're Getting Stuff to the Event">
    You can hand-carry items, ship to the FedEx Office on-site, or ship to the Advance Warehouse. Restrictions and deadlines apply to each option, so plan accordingly.

    See [Shipping Overview](/shipping/overview).
  </Step>  
    <Step title="Register Your Delegates">
    Use the Partner Hub to tell us who will be attending from your organization. Partners receive complimentary delegate registrations based on their level of participation, and additional registrations are available for $70 each.

    See [Registering Your Delegates](/booth/attendee-giveaways).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Promote Your Presence">
    Tell the community you're attending—and continually remind them as the event gets closer. The most successful exhibitors offer unique experiences on-site with demos, giveaways, product launches, and other engaging content.

    See [Promote Your Presence](/booth/promote-your-presence).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Arrive for Load-In">
    Booth setup begins **Wednesday, June 9 at 3pm** and must be complete by **4pm on Friday, June 11** when the exhibit hall opens to the public.

    See the full [Event Schedule](/schedule/event-schedule).
  </Step>
</Steps>
