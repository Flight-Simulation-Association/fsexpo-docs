Short on time? This page covers the handful of things every exhibitor needs to take care of before June. Each step links to the full details if you need them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Order your booth add-ons">
    Use the Partner Hub to see what's included in your booth (size, furnishings, internet, electrical, monitors) and order anything extra. **Deadline: Friday, May 29.**

    See [Booth Specs & Equipment](/booth/specs-and-equipment).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide how you're getting your stuff there">
    Hand-carry it, ship it to the loading dock, or ship it to the advance warehouse. The advance warehouse is usually the easiest option, but it has its own shipping window.

    See [Shipping Overview](/shipping/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ship to the advance warehouse (if that's your plan)">
    Items must arrive between **May 12 and May 29** to avoid a late fee, with an absolute deadline of **June 8**.

    See [Advance Warehouse Shipping](/shipping/advance-warehouse).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book your hotel and travel">
    FlightSimExpo has negotiated discounts on hotels, air travel, and ground transportation.

    See [Hotel & Travel Discounts](/travel/hotel-and-travel-discounts).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plan your giveaway items">
    Recommended quantity for 2026 is **1,500 items**. Shipped giveaways need special addressing — email phil@flightsimassociation.com for instructions.

    See [Attendee Giveaways](/booth/attendee-giveaways).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join us on Discord">
    Post announcements, share your plans, or offer giveaways and discounts directly to attendees.

    See [Join us in Discord](/get-involved/discord).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show up for load-in">
    Booth setup opens **Wednesday, June 10 at 3:00pm** and must be complete by **4:30pm Friday, June 12** when the hall opens to the public.

    See the full [Event Schedule](/schedule/event-schedule).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Want every date in one place instead? Check [Key Dates & Deadlines](/schedule/key-dates).
</Note>
