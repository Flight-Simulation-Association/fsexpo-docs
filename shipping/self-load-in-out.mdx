During load-in and load-out, exhibitors can access the loading dock with their own vehicle **at no charge,** subject to the restrictions below.

Exhibitors who choose to self load-in/out must be self-sufficient: you are responsible for bringing all equipment, labor, and other items needed to load/unload, complete setup, and store empty boxes/crates.

## Restrictions on Self Load-In/Out

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Bonafide Employees Only" icon="id-badge">
    Personnel performing the work must be full-time employees of the exhibiting company.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hand-Operated Equipment Only" icon="cart-flatbed">
    Self-supplied, two-wheeled hand trucks and four-wheeled flat carts are permitted.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No Fork Trucks" icon="ban">
    The use of forklifts or other mechanized equipment is restricted to licensed personnel.
  </Card>
  <Card title="You Own Your Storage" icon="box">
    You assume responsibility for storing empty boxes/creates during the show to ensure they aren't disposed of.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Instructions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Park at the Loading Dock">
  <Note>
    Access to the loading dock is available on a first-come, first-serve basis.
  </Note>
    You may load in any time after **3pm on Wednesday, June 9.** The loading dock address is:
    ```
    310 Eagle Street
    Saint Paul, MN 55102
    ```
    Upon arrival, personnel from the venue and/or Demers (our show services partner) will direct you where to park and unload.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manually Unload Your Items">
    Two-wheeled hand trucks and four-wheeled flat carts are permitted, but must be self-supplied. Equipment and labor assistance will not be provided.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Relocate Your Vehicle">
    After unloading, relocate your vehicle to the parking lot.

    To ensure sufficient space exists for other exhibitors to load-in, **relocate your vehicle immediately after you've finished unloading and before beginning to setup your booth.**
    <Note>
      Vehicles in the loading dock area must be actively loading or unloading, and then must relocate to the parking lot. Long-term or overnight parking in the loading dock is not permitted.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Load-Out
Load-out follows the same process as above, and is subject to the same restrictions.

<Info>
  Dock access for load out begins at **3pm on Sunday.** Vehicles are not permitted to stage early, while the event is still open to the public.
</Info>
