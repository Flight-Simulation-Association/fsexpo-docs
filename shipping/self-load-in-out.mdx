During load-in and load-out, exhibitors can access the loading dock with their own vehicle at no charge. Upon arrival, personnel from the venue and Demers (our show services partner) will direct you where to park and unload.

<Note>
  Access to the loading dock is available on a first-come, first-serve basis. Additionally, dock access for load out does not begin until **3pm on Sunday.** Vehicles are not permitted to stage prior for load-out.
</Note>

## Restrictions on Self Load-In/Out

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Bonafide Employees Only" icon="id-badge">
    Personnel performing the work must be full-time employees of the exhibiting company.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hand-Operated Equipment Only" icon="cart-flatbed">
    Two-wheeled hand trucks and four-wheeled flat carts are permitted, but must be self-supplied.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No Fork Trucks" icon="ban">
    The use of forklifts or other mechanized equipment is restricted to licensed personnel.
  </Card>
  <Card title="You Own Your Storage" icon="box">
    You assume responsibility for storing empty boxes during the show and ensuring they aren't disposed of.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
