Demers can assist with inbound and outbound freight handling, including:

- Receiving your items in advance and delivering them directly to your booth
- Storage of empty boxes/crates during show hours
- Return of empty boxes/crates during move-out
- Facilitation of outbound shipping

## Shipping window

<Warning>
  Items must be **received between May 12 and May 29**. Items received after May 29 are subject to a late fee of at least $100, with an **absolute deadline of Monday, June 8**.
</Warning>

Shipments may be received **Monday–Friday, 8:30am–3:30pm**, excluding holidays. You can use your own carrier, a courier like FedEx or UPS, or ABF Freight (Demers' preferred carrier — email ashley@demers-av.com for a quote).

## Shipping label

All materials must be labeled and addressed as follows:

```
To: [Name of Exhibitor] & Booth #
FlightSimExpo c/o ABF Freight Inc.
2950 Lone Oak Cir
Eagan, MN 55121, United States of America
```

## Package handling rates

| Package | Handling charge |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 3 small boxes, ≤50 lbs. each | $50 per box |
| All other items | $105 per 100 lb. increment, $210 minimum |
