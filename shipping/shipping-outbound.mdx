<Note>
  These options are for **large shipments:** crates, pallets, or anything that can't be hand-carried off the show floor. For small boxes and packages, see [FedEx Office](/shipping/fedex-office). To hand-carry items yourself, see [Self Load-In/Out](/shipping/self-load-in-out).
</Note>

There are two ways to ship large items outbound, depending on whether your carrier can pick up on Sunday.

## Ship from the Loading Dock

If your carrier can pick up directly from the show site on Sunday, or if you are putting the items into your own vehicle, Demers will move your shipment to the loading dock and you or your carrier take it from there.

### Material Handling Charges
Inbound material handling charges cover outbound service. If you did not ship to the Advance Warehouse or use Demers inbound, the charge is **\$1.35/lb with a minimum charge of $95.**

### Instructions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Arrange a Sunday Pickup with your Carrier">
    Make arrangements with your carrier to pick up directly from the venue loading dock on Sunday afternoon. **Carriers must check in with Demers on-site by 5pm.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-Pack Your Items">
    Once the event ends, re-pack your pallets and large shipments at your booth. Then, notify Demers that the items are ready for pickup. Demers will move the items to the loading dock and your carrier will pick them up from there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ship Outbound via Demers

If your carrier cannot pick up on Sunday, Demers can take your shipment back to their warehouse. You then schedule a pick up from the warehouse with your carrier at a later date.

### Material Handling Charges
\$1.35/lb. with a minimum charge of $95.

### Instructions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete a Bill of Lading">
    Visit the Demers Service Desk on-site prior to show close to complete a bill of lading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-Pack Your Items">
    Once the event ends, re-pack your pallets and large shipments at your booth. Then, notify Demers that the items are ready for pickup. Demers will transport the items back to their warehouse.
    <Info>
      Verify your pallet is correctly shrink-wrapped and has the bill of landing affixed to it before departing. We recommend taking photos of the packed pallet for documentation purposes, should it later become damaged during shipping. 
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule a Pickup">
    Schedule a pickup with your preferred carrier **on or after July 1** from the Demers warehouse address:
    ```
    Demers Events & Expo Services
    151A Park Ave
    East Hartford, CT 06108
    ```
    Advance Warehouse pick up hours are **Monday–Friday, 8:30am–3:30pm**, excluding holidays. Ensure your carrier only attempts pick up during these hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>
