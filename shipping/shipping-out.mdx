FlightSimExpo 2026 ends at 3:00pm on Sunday. All materials must be removed from the facility by **9:00pm on Sunday, June 14**.

<Warning>
  Anything left in the facility after 9:00pm will be **disposed of at your expense**.
</Warning>

You can take items with you, ship them outbound, or a combination of both.

## Take items with you

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="A · Hand-Carry Off the Floor">
    **How it works:** Hand-carry your items — mechanical assistance is limited to a self-supplied small dolly (several restrictions apply). Dollies will not be provided.

    **Material handling charges:** None.

    **Instructions:** Park and load in the Exhibitor Load-Out Area during move-out hours — see [Self Load-In/Out](/shipping/self-load-in-out).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="B · Pick Up at the Loading Dock">
    **How it works:** Demers' personnel move your items from the show floor and load your vehicle at the loading dock.

    **Material handling charges:** Covered by the fees paid at load-in.

    **Instructions:** Coordinate with Demers once on-site.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Ship items out (Option C)

There are four ways to ship items outbound — pick the one that matches your situation.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="C-1 · Small shipments (up to 3 boxes, ≤50 lbs each) via FedEx or UPS">
    **How it works:** Label items and leave them at the Demers Service Desk. Demers brings them to their warehouse; you schedule a FedEx or UPS pickup on or after **June 22**.

    **Material handling charges:** Inbound charges cover outbound service. If you didn't ship to Demers inbound, the outbound charge is **$50 per package**.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Complete a bill of lading">At the Demers Service Desk on-site, prior to show close.</Step>
      <Step title="Label your package">From address: 151A Park Ave, East Hartford, CT 06108.</Step>
      <Step title="Schedule pickup">Starting June 22, with FedEx or UPS. Warehouse hours: 8:30am–3:30pm Monday–Friday, excluding holidays.</Step>
    </Steps>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="C-2 · Large shipments from the show site on Sunday">
    **How it works:** Have your own carrier pick up directly from the show site on **Sunday, June 14**, or use the house carrier (ABF Freight) to ship that day.

    **Material handling charges:** None.

    - Arrange with your carrier to pick up directly from the venue on Sunday, June 14. Carriers must check in with Demers by **5:00pm**.
    - If your carrier can't pick up Sunday, use the house carrier (ABF Freight) — email ashley@demers-av.com for a quote.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="C-3 · Large shipments from the post-event warehouse">
    **How it works:** If your carrier can't pick up from the show site on Sunday, Demers takes your shipment back to their warehouse. Schedule pickup with your preferred carrier starting **June 22**.

    **Material handling charges:** $1.35/lb., $95 minimum.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Complete a bill of lading">At the Demers Service Desk on-site, prior to show close.</Step>
      <Step title="Schedule pickup">Starting June 22, from the Demers warehouse: 151A Park Ave, East Hartford, CT 06108. Warehouse hours: 8:30am–3:30pm Monday–Friday, excluding holidays.</Step>
    </Steps>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="C-4 · Leave items for Monday pickup at the venue">
    **How it works:** Leave items on the Saint Paul RiverCentre loading dock, at your own risk, for pickup on **Monday, June 15** by your preferred carrier.

    **Material handling charges:** None.

    <Warning>
      There is **no overnight security** on Sunday night. You're responsible for meeting your carrier on Monday — items not picked up will be disposed of at your expense. FlightSimExpo and Demers are not responsible for damaged or missing items left this way.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
