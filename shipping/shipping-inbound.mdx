<Note>
  These options are for **large shipments:** crates, pallets, or anything that can't be hand-carried onto the show floor. For small boxes and packages, see [FedEx Office](/shipping/fedex-office). To hand-carry items yourself, see [Self Load-In/Out](/shipping/self-load-in-out).
</Note>

## Ship to the Loading Dock

If you have large items that need help getting from the loading dock to your booth, Demers can assist—whether that's a freight shipment, or your own truck that needs unloading.

<Danger>
  This option is only suitable if your freight carrier can deliver to the loading dock **while you are on-site**, so you're there to meet the shipment. Shipping to the loading dock **in advance is not recommended.** The dock is unmonitored, so anything delivered ahead of time sits unattended until you arrive, and is at your own risk. 
</Danger>

### Material Handling Charges
\$95 per 100 lb. with a minimum charge of $190.

### Instructions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ship Your Items">
    All materials must be labeled and addressed as follows:
    ```
    To: [Name of Exhibitor] & Booth #
    FlightSimExpo c/o ABF Freight Inc.
    2950 Lone Oak Cir
    Eagan, MN 55121, USA
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Time Delivery to Your Arrival">
    Coordinate with your carrier so the shipment arrives at the loading dock while you are on-site. Meet your carrier at the loading dock.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Coordinate with Your Carrier and Demers On-Site">
    Once the carrier arrives, coordinate with Demers to unload the shipment and move it to your booth.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Ship to the Advance Warehouse

Shipping to the Advance Warehouse includes full end-to-end handling of your items by Demers. They will:

- Receive your items in advance and deliver them directly to your booth prior to setup
- Store empty boxes/crates during show hours
- Return empty boxes/crates to your booth during load-out
- Facilitate outbound shipping

### Receiving Window
Items must be **received between May 12 and May 29**. Items received after May 29 are subject to a late fee of at least $100, with an absolute deadline of Monday, June 8.

Advance Warehouse receiving hours are **Monday–Friday, 8:30am–3:30pm**, excluding holidays. Ensure your carrier only attempts delivery during these hours.

### Material Handling Charges
\$105 per 100 lb. increment, with a minimum charge of $210.
