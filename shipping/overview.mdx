You can bring items with you, ship them in advance, or use a combination of both. This page compares your **inbound** options — see [Shipping Out](/shipping/shipping-out) for getting items home afterward.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Hand-Carry It Yourself">
    **How it works:** Bring items with you and hand-carry them onto the exhibit floor. Mechanical assistance is limited to a self-supplied small dolly.

    **Timeline:** Any time after 3:00pm on Wednesday, June 10.

    **Material handling charges:** None.

    **Instructions:** Park in the Exhibitor Load-In Area — see [Self Load-In/Out](/shipping/self-load-in-out).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Ship to the Loading Dock">
    **How it works:** Ship or drop off items, with load-in assistance from Demers on-site staff.

    **Timeline:** Arrive during Show Services Staff Hours — see the [Event Schedule](/schedule/event-schedule).

    **Material handling charges:** $95 per 100 lb. increment, $190 minimum.

    **Instructions:** Contact Ashley Parker (ashley@demers-av.com) to coordinate load-in timing and get a material handling estimate. Provide a credit card in advance to avoid additional admin fees.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Ship to the Advance Warehouse">
    **How it works:** Ship to Demers' warehouse ahead of the show. Items are delivered directly to your booth on **Wednesday, June 10**. Empty boxes are stored and returned to you at the end of the show for easy load-out.

    **Timeline:** Arrive between **May 12 and May 29**.

    **Material handling charges:**
    - Over 50 lbs: $95 per 100 lb. increment, $190 minimum
    - Individual boxes under 50 lbs: $50 each, max 3 boxes

    **Instructions:** See [Advance Warehouse Shipping](/shipping/advance-warehouse) for the full process, address, and label format.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  Most exhibitors find the advance warehouse option easiest — it avoids coordinating a specific load-in time and gets items placed at your booth before you even arrive.
</Note>
