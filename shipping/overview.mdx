You can bring items with you, ship them, or use a mix of both. Restrictions and deadlines apply to each option, so determine what works best for your situation.

## Inbound Options

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Self Load-In" icon="dolly" href="/shipping/self-load-in-out">
    Bring items with you and hand-carry them onto the show floor yourself.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ship to the On-Site FedEx Office" icon="box-isometric-tape" href="/shipping/fedex-office">
    Pick up your packages and hand-carry them onto the show floor yourself.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ship to the Loading Dock" icon="truck" href="/shipping/shipping-inbound">
    Ship or drop off large items that cannot be hand-carried. Then, have Demers move them to your booth.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ship to the Advance Warehouse" icon="warehouse" href="/shipping/shipping-inbound">
    Ship items to Demers' warehouse in advance. They will be delivered directly to your booth before you arrive.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Outbound Options

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Self Load-Out" icon="dolly" href="/shipping/self-load-in-out">
    Hand-carry your items off the show floor and into your own vehicle.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ship from the On-Site FedEx Office" icon="box-isometric-tape" href="/shipping/fedex-office">
    Drop off small boxes and packages with prepaid labels for shipping outbound.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ship from the Loading Dock" icon="truck-ramp-box" href="/shipping/shipping-outbound">
    Have Demers move large items to the loading dock for pickup by yourself or your own carrier on Sunday afternoon.
  </Card>
  
  <Card title="Ship Outbound via Demers" icon="box" href="shipping/shipping-outbound">
    Have Demers take large items back to their warehouse for pickup by any carrier at a later date.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
