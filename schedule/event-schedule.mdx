<Steps>
  <Step title="Wednesday, June 10 — Booth setup begins (3:00pm)">
    Proceed to FlightSimExpo Exhibitor Check-In in the Kellogg Lobby of the Saint Paul RiverCentre. Registered delegates receive badges, and exhibitors (and anyone under their escort) can access the Exhibit Hall continuously, including outside show hours, with their badge.

    A contracted security officer is on-site starting at 6:00pm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Friday, June 12 — Setup deadline & show opens (4:30pm)">
    Exhibit setup must be **complete by 4:30pm**, when the Exhibit Hall opens to the public. Setup is not permitted before show hours on Saturday and Sunday, aside from routine tasks like powering on equipment or basic tidying.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Friday–Sunday, June 12–14 — Show days">
    FlightSimExpo 2026 is open to attendees.

    <Tip>
      Attendees have told us they notice when exhibitors pack up early. Exhibitors who vacate their booth before 3:00pm on Sunday may be subject to penalties at future events — see [Feedback & Tips](/get-involved/feedback-and-tips).
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sunday, June 14 — Show closes (3:00pm)">
    Outbound carriers must check in with Demers by **5:00pm** if picking up directly from the show site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sunday, June 14 — All materials out (9:00pm)">
    Everything must be removed from the facility by 9:00pm. See [Shipping Out](/shipping/shipping-out) for your pickup/ship-out options.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Venue policies restrict FlightSimExpo volunteers from assisting with booth setup — plan your own staffing for setup and booth coverage. We're happy to help arrange assistance in advance if you need it.
</Note>
