## Arriving On-Site
Proceed to FlightSimExpo Exhibitor Check-In in the Paradise Event Center Foyer of the Westgate Las Vegas. Registered delegates receive badges, and exhibitors (and anyone under their escort) can access the Exhibit Hall continuously, including outside show hours, with their badge.

<Note>
  Venue policies restrict FlightSimExpo volunteers from assisting with booth setup, so plan accordingly. We're happy to help arrange assistance in advance if you need it.
</Note>
