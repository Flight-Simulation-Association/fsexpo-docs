description: "How to tell real FlightSimExpo communication from fraud"
icon: "shield-halved"
---

We take great care in protecting the contact information you provide to us, and do not share it with third parties, except:

1. Through the FlightSimExpo Partner Directory, if you opt in.
2. With Demers Events & Expo Services, our show services partner — they may contact you with questions about your shipping plans or add-on orders.

Otherwise, all legitimate communication will come directly from the FlightSimExpo team with an `@flightsimassociation.com` or `@flightsimexpo.com` address.

<Warning>
  **This is a common scam that affects nearly every event.** In one instance in 2024, a caller claiming to be "from the FlightSimExpo hotel" attempted to obtain an exhibitor's credit card number by falsely claiming the hotel rate had changed. Some fraudulent emails even use the FSExpo logo illegally. Search "attendee list sales scams" for more examples of this pattern across the events industry.
</Warning>

We anticipate that partners will receive unsolicited emails or phone calls from companies falsely offering to sell contact information, hotel booking services, or exhibitor/attendee lists for our conference.

<Card title="What to do" icon="circle-check">
  Emails and phone calls about FlightSimExpo from anyone other than our team or Demers Events & Expo Services are fraudulent and should be ignored. Mark them as spam and/or report them.

  **FlightSimExpo will never call you unsolicited to request credit card or other financial information.**
</Card>

Not sure if something is legitimate? Reach out to your FlightSimExpo representative or [contact us](/#contact-us) directly to confirm.
