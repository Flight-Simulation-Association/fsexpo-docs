You can take items with you, ship them, or use a mix of both. Restrictions apply to each option, so determine what works best for your situation.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Self Load-Out" icon="dolly" href="/outbound-shipping/self-load-out">
    Best for exhibitors who are driving their own vehicle and equpiment home from Westgate Las Vegas.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ship from the On-Site FedEx Office" icon="box-isometric-tape" href="/outbound-shipping/fedex-office">
    Best for small boxes and packages with prepaid labels that can be dropped off and shipped outbound.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ship from the Loading Dock" icon="truck-ramp-box" href="/outbound-shipping/loading-dock">
    Best for large shipments picked up by yourself or your own carrier on Sunday afternoon.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ship Outbound via the Warehouse" icon="box" href="/outbound-shipping/warehouse">
    Best for large shipments that cannot be picked up on Sunday afternoon.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
