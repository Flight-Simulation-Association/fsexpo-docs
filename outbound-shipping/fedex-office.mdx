<Note>
  Shipping from the FedEx Office is only suitable for small boxes or packages that can be hand-carried from your booth. The FedEx Office is not equipped to handle large shipments like pallets or crates.
</Note>

A FedEx Office is located on-site at Westgate Las Vegas for your convenience. It can be used to ship small boxes and packages outbound, provided they have prepaid labels.

## Shipping Instructions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Purchase Prepaid FedEx Labels">
    Labels can be purchased from FedEx Office during business hours, or online at any time.
    <Info>
      Plan ahead! Ensure you have a printer, packing tape, boxes, etc. or visit FedEx Office during business hours to purchase these items. 
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pack Your Items">
    After the show ends, pack your shipments appropriately, and then place the prepaid shipping labels on your packages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drop Off Your Items at FedEx Office">
    Note that items can only be dropped off during FedEx Office's business hours: **Monday-Friday, X-Y.**
  </Step>
</Steps>
