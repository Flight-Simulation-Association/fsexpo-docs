The exhibit hall is restricted to FlightSimExpo attendees, sponsors, exhibitors, and personnel identified by FlightSimExpo badges. Members of the public who aren't registered generally won't have access to event spaces — though the Saint Paul RiverCentre is a public facility, so other groups may be in nearby meeting spaces.

## After-hours access

Public access to the venue is locked down between **11:00pm and 6:00am** daily. During these hours, entrance to the facility is restricted to FlightSimExpo exhibitors wearing their badge, via a separate loading dock entrance. Additional details are provided on-site for anyone needing access during these hours.
