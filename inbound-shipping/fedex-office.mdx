<Note>
  Shipping to the FedEx Office is only suitable for small boxes or packages that can be hand-carried to your booth. The FedEx Office is not equipped to handle large shipments like pallets or crates, and there is no mechanism to get such shipments onto the show floor.
</Note>

A FedEx Office is located on-site at Westgate Las Vegas for your convenience. In addition to receiving small boxes and packages, it can be used for last-minute printing services.

## Receiving Window
Items must be **received between May 12 and May 29**. Items received after May 29 are subject to a late fee of at least $100, with an absolute deadline of Monday, June 8.

FedEx Office receiving hours are **Monday–Friday, 8:30am–3:30pm**, excluding holidays. Ensure your carrier only attempts delivery during these hours.

## Material Handling Charges

| Package | Handling Charge |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 3 small boxes, ≤50 lbs. each | $50 per box |
| All other items | \$105 per 100 lb. increment, $210 minimum |

    ```
    FlightSimExpo c/o ABF Freight Inc.
    2950 Lone Oak Cir
    Eagan, MN 55121, USA
    ```
