<Note>
  This option is only suitable for large shipments that are **delivered to the loading dock during specified load-in hours**. Shipping to the loading dock in advance is not permitted. The dock is unmonitored, so anything delivered outside of load-in hours sits unattended, at your own risk. 
</Note>

If you have large items that need help getting from the loading dock to your booth, Demers will move them for you—whether that's a freight shipment, or your own truck that needs unloading.

## Receiving Window
Items must be received during the following hours. Ensure your carrier only attempts delivery during these hours.

-	**Wednesday, June 9:** 3pm – 5pm
-	**Thursday, June 10:** 9am – 5pm 
-	**Friday, June 11:** 9am – 12pm

### Material Handling Charges
\$95 per 100 lb. with a minimum charge of $190.

### Instructions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ship or Drop Off Your Items">
    The loading dock address is:
    ```
    310 Eagle Street
    Saint Paul, MN 55102
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ensure Delivery During Receiving Hours">
    Coordinate with your carrier so the shipment arrives at the loading dock during the receiving hours noted above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Coordinate with Your Carrier and Demers On-Site">
    Meet your carrier at the loading dock and coordinate with Demers to unload the shipment and move it to your booth.
  </Step>
</Steps>
