You can bring items with you, ship them, or use a mix of both. Restrictions and deadlines apply to each option, so determine what works best for your situation.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Self Load-In" icon="dolly" href="/inbound-shipping/self-load-in">
    Best for exhibitors who are driving their own vehicle and equipment to Westgate Las Vegas.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ship to the On-Site FedEx Office" icon="box-isometric-tape" href="/inbound-shipping/fedex-office">
    Best for small boxes and packages that you can pick-up and hand-carry onto the show floor yourself.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ship to the Loading Dock" icon="truck-ramp-box" href="/inbound-shipping/loading-dock">
    Best for large shipments arriving during specified load-in hours that cannot be hand-carried to your booth.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ship to the Advance Warehouse" icon="warehouse" href="/inbound-shipping/warehouse">
    Best for large shipments that should be delivered directly to your booth before you arrive.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
