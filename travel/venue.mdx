## Saint Paul RiverCentre

```
175 West Kellogg Boulevard
Saint Paul, MN
```

Registered delegates check in at **FlightSimExpo Exhibitor Check-In**, located in the Kellogg Lobby.

## Loading dock

```
310 Eagle Street
Saint Paul, MN 55102
```

See [Self Load-In/Out](/shipping/self-load-in-out) for load-in/load-out rules at this entrance.

<Note>
  Public access to the venue is locked down between **11:00pm and 6:00am** daily. During these hours, entrance is restricted to FlightSimExpo exhibitors wearing their badge, via the loading dock entrance. See [Security & Access](/on-site/security-and-access) for details.
</Note>
