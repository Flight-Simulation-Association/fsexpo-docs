FlightSimExpo attendees shared the following feedback in post-event surveys. It's provided here to help you get the most out of your time as an exhibitor.

<Card title='Watch our "Maximizing Your ROI at FlightSimExpo" session' icon="video" horizontal>
  Prior to the show, we host a free seminar for partners covering feedback from past events and best practices. Look for a calendar invitation in March or April as the event approaches.
</Card>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Ability to buy on-site" icon="cart-shopping">
    The most frequent comment we hear from attendees! Consider pre-sales with on-site pickup, point-of-sale transactions with shipping to the attendee's home, or bringing merchandise with you. If you sell virtual items, sell download codes right on-site.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Offer show-specific discounts" icon="tag">
    Attendees would like to see discounts offered to those in attendance, as is common at other industry events.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Health practices" icon="hand-sparkles">
    Attendees were concerned about the spread of germs, especially when trying simulators or VR headsets. Consider wipes to clean VR headsets between uses, or single-use covers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Announce new products" icon="star">
    Attendees are looking for exhibitors and developers to announce new products. Use shows like FlightSimExpo as a platform to "wow the audience," rather than just updating existing work.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Stay for the whole event" icon="clock">
    Attendees noticed exhibitors packing up before 3:00pm on Sunday, and said it hurt the "vibe" toward the end of the show. Exhibitors who vacate their booth before 3:00pm on Sunday may be subject to penalties at future events.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Consider reservations or tickets for your booth" icon="ticket">
    For popular displays, a "take a number" or sign-up system lets attendees walk the floor while waiting their turn. This is especially useful for higher-end hardware vendors who want one-on-one time with seriously interested customers.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
